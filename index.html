Common questions

How do I know which Microsoft 365 license is right for my business?

It depends on how many users you have, how they work, and your security and compliance needs. In the consultation we review your usage and recommend right-sized licenses.

Can you migrate us from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365?

Yes. We handle Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 migrations as well as tenant-to-tenant migrations between Microsoft 365 accounts.

How long does a migration take?

It depends on the number of users, the volume of mail and files, and how complex your setup is. We give you a timeline after we understand your environment.

Will my team face downtime?

We plan migrations in stages to keep disruption low, and we agree the cutover plan with you in advance.

What is M365ProCare?

M365ProCare is our 24/7 managed service for Microsoft 365, giving your team ongoing support after you go live.
